# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique ideas and code, explain
disagreements with evidence, and welcome people with different experience levels.
Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, and posting private data are
not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to provide unpaid work or make
promises on behalf of the project.

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's reporting tools; do not repeat private
details in a public issue. Maintainers may moderate discussions and restrict
participation. Participation does not entitle anyone to repository access.
